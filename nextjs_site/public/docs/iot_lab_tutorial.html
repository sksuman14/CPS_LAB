Step-by-Step User Guide

sensors

Welcome to the Lab!

The IoT Sensor Lab allows you to connect physical hardware sensors to your browser, write custom logic using visual blocks, and see live data in real-time. Follow the steps below to run your first sensor experiment.

1

Connect Your Hardware

Plug your sensor kit into your computer using a USB cable. Make sure the device is powered on.

  • Click the Connect button (if prompted) or rely on the auto-connection feature.
  • Your browser will ask for permission to connect to a Serial Port.
  • Select your device (often labeled as USB Serial CH340 or CP210x) and click Connect.
[Screenshot: Browser popup showing "USB Serial Device" selection]
2

Select Your Sensor from the Kit

In the "Your Sensor Kit" panel, click on the specific sensor you have connected (e.g., AHT20, Soil Spectra, Rain Gauge).

[Screenshot: The horizontal list of sensors with one highlighted in blue]
3

Write Logic or Load a Template

Use the Blockly Editor on the left side of the screen to drag and drop logic blocks.

  • You can build custom logic (e.g., "If temperature > 30, trigger alert").
  • Alternatively, use the Load Template... dropdown in the top right toolbar to load a pre-built example for your specific sensor.
[Screenshot: Blockly canvas showing a simple "On Sensor Data" block]
4

Run the Experiment

Once your blocks are ready, click the green Play (Run) button in the top toolbar.

1. Live Sensor Data: Watch the black terminal window on the bottom right. You will see raw data streaming directly from your physical sensor in real-time.
2. Visual Feedback: If your blocks include animations or custom UI widgets, they will appear dynamically in the dashboard panel above the terminal!
5

Stop and Disconnect

When you are finished observing the data:

  • Click the red Stop button to halt your logic.
  • Click the Unplug button (or simply unplug your USB cable) to safely disconnect the hardware.
